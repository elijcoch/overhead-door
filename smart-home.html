What should I check first?

Confirm whether the wall button and handheld remote still operate the door normally. Note whether only the phone connection is affected, and use the exact product’s owner instructions.

What information helps the team?

The opener model, accessory model if present, app name, phone operating system, when the issue started, and any visible error message. Share a safely taken label photo if available.

Where can I control my connected garage door?

Use the manufacturer’s supported app to operate your door and manage account access. Our team can help you find compatible products, instructions, and setup support.

Should I reset my account or erase paired controls?

Read the model instructions first. Resetting may remove controls or access settings. If you’re unsure, ask for help before changing them.
