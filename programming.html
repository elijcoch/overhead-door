Before you start

A little preparation.
Less frustration.

Do I need to enter my PIN or password here?

No. Never enter or send garage access codes, account passwords, or Wi-Fi passwords in a service request.

What if the door is damaged or moves unevenly?

Stop operating it and request service. Programming a control will not repair a spring, cable, track, or balance problem.

Why don’t all remotes use the same steps?

The control model, opener generation, and radio system determine compatibility and pairing. Match both products to the manufacturer instructions.

Can I use these instructions for a commercial operator?

Use the instructions for the exact commercial operator and controls. Ask the commercial service team about access permissions, installation, and operational requirements.
