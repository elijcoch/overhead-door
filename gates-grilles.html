What if I don’t know the system type?

Select “Not sure.” Explain what you can see from a safe location. Measurements and model numbers are optional.

Where does my request go?

Gate and grille requests are prepared for hoyt@odcamarillo.com. Other service requests go to cody@odcamarillo.com. You send the prepared message in your email app.

What if the entrance is blocked?

Keep people clear and call our office. Describe whether the gate is open, closed, or partly open and whether vehicles or business access are blocked.
