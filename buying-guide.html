Understand the differences

Good questions.
Clearer choices.

R-value or U-factor: which is better?

R-value describes resistance to heat flow; higher values indicate greater resistance. U-factor describes heat transfer; lower values indicate less transfer. Ratings may refer to different parts of a system, so compare equivalent test scopes rather than treating them as interchangeable.

Can I add windows or paint the door later?

Options, approved finishes, and modifications vary by model. Check the applicable manufacturer documents and warranty before making changes.

Should I replace the opener with the door?

It depends on condition, capacity, compatibility, and features you want. Have the complete system reviewed before deciding.

Will a new door increase my home’s value?

A garage door can improve curb appeal and daily use. Resale value varies by home, market, and project cost; an industry average is not a guaranteed return.

How do I check product coverage?

Use the warranty document for the exact model and purchase period. Manufacturer product coverage and installer labor coverage may differ.
