Ask our team
What service coverage do you provide?

Our service terms include 30 days of labor coverage on service calls and one year on items we sell and install. Contact our office to confirm the current terms and coverage for your job.

Is a manufacturer warranty the same as installation coverage?

Product coverage and installer labor coverage can differ. Use the applicable document and ask our team about the work performed on your job.

What should I include with a warranty question?

Share the model, purchase or installation date, installer, and the issue. Include proof of purchase and photos in your email if available.

Does downloading a document confirm my coverage?

No. Coverage depends on the terms for your particular product and circumstances. The document is the starting point for that conversation.
