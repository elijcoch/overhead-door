Common questions

A little clarity
goes a long way.

Straightforward answers to help you choose your next step.

Do you repair other brands?

Yes. We service what we sell and most other makes and models of doors and openers. Tell us the brand if you know it; it’s fine if you don’t.

Do I need measurements to request help?

No. For a new door, opening width and height are useful if you already know them. For repairs, the symptoms and current door position are usually more useful. Our technician can verify dimensions.

Does a request book an appointment?

No. A request starts the conversation. Our office will confirm the details, availability, and any service charges before an appointment is arranged.

Can I visit the showroom?

Yes. Visit 4111 Northwest 10th Avenue in Amarillo, Monday–Friday, 7:30 am–4:30 pm. Call 806-372-5884 if you’d like to discuss a specific product.

Can one damaged section be replaced?

Sometimes. Availability depends on the door model, condition, and extent of the damage. A technician can recommend a section replacement or a complete door.

What should I do if the door is crooked or off track?

Keep people clear and stop operating it. Do not touch springs or cables, or force the door. Call our office and describe what you can see from a safe distance.

Can I paint a garage door?

Many doors can be painted, but the finish and warranty requirements vary by model. Check the correct owner’s manual or ask our team before choosing paint.

What sizes are available?

Common residential doors are 7 or 8 feet tall; widths and custom sizes depend on the product series and opening. Share your opening dimensions if known so our team can help identify options.

What does insulation R-value mean?

R-value describes resistance to heat flow. Compare the manufacturer’s ratings and construction details for the specific models you’re considering, along with your garage’s intended use.

Can a door work with a low ceiling?

Low-headroom track and opener options may be available. The correct solution depends on the door, hardware, and clearances. Our team can measure the opening and confirm a compatible system.

Can an existing door be insulated?

An insulation upgrade may be possible for some doors. Ask our team to check the model, panel construction, added weight, and balance before selecting a kit.

How often should a garage door be inspected?

Arrange a professional inspection annually and ask for help sooner if the door becomes noisy, uneven, damaged, or unreliable. Follow your model’s maintenance instructions.

Where can I find technical documents?

Use our download center for product brochures, warranties, owner manuals, specifications, CAD drawings, and BIM models.
